### 🧬 About the Organization
We specialize in the **architectural design of high-fidelity backtesting environments** and the provision of **institutional-grade market data**. Our mission is to bridge the gap between raw exchange data and actionable quantitative insights through optimized data structures and rigorous statistical processing.

- 🔭 **Current Focus**: Scaling open-source 1-minute OHLCV datasets for Binance.
- ⚙️ **Engineering**: High-performance data pipelines using Apache Parquet and PyArrow.
- 📈 **Goal**: Providing the cleanest, most reliable data for algorithmic strategy validation.
